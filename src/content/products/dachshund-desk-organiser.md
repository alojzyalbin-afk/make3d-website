A dachshund desk organiser that grows to the length you want. The head and collar are at one end, the tail is at the other and in between you choose how many compartments you need for pens, brushes, scissors, keys or small plants. Add a phone compartment with a slot and a charging hole if you want your phone to stand next to the dog.

Your dog's name goes on the collar tag and you choose the colours. I 3D print every organiser to order in Dublin and put it together by hand.

### What you choose

- Number of compartments: 1 to 4. The dog gets 38 mm longer with each one
- Phone compartment: none or one, with a hole for the charging cable
- Colours: body, accent, collar, name tag and tag lettering
- The name on the collar tag

### Details

- The starting set is the head, collar, chest, front legs, 1 compartment, the rear with the tail and the back legs
- Each compartment is 38 mm long. The opening at the top is about 33 mm long and 36 mm wide, then it widens to about 55 mm inside and is about 64 mm deep. Each one holds roughly 0.1 litre
- The phone compartment is 56 mm long. The slot is 14 mm wide, leans back by 15 degrees and holds a phone up to about 13 mm thick with its case. A 12 x 9 mm hole in the bottom of the slot lets the charging cable go down and out under the dog's belly. Behind the slot there is a small pocket about 17 mm long
- Length from nose to tail: approx. 247 mm with 1 compartment, 285 mm with 2, 323 mm with 3 and 361 mm with 4. The phone compartment adds 56 mm
- Height: approx. 167 mm. Width: approx. 75 mm at the widest point. The dog stands on four legs and its belly is 24 mm above the desk
- Collar tag: 24 x 13 mm, with the name in raised letters
- PLA plastic. The colour comes from the plastic itself, with no paint. The eyes and the nose are always black
- The parts are joined with hidden pins and glue, so it arrives in one piece
- The phone charger and cable are not included
- 3D printed and put together by me in Dublin, Ireland

### About the design

The shape of the dog started from a 3D model made with AI tools. I rebuilt it in CAD and designed the compartments, the way the parts fit together, the collar and the name tag myself.

### Colours

Body colour: head, chest, legs and rear. Accent colour: jaw, ears, eyebrows and the compartments. The collar, the name tag and the tag lettering each get their own colour.

Pick from: White, Cream, Black, Grey, Navy Blue, Blue, Sky Blue, Mint Green, Grass Green, Mustard Green, Yellow, Mandarin Orange, Red, Pink, Desert Tan, Latte Brown, Brown or Gold

Shown in the photos: grey body, tan accent, orange collar and a cream tag with red lettering.

### How to order

1. Choose the number of compartments, with or without the phone compartment, in the order form.
2. Type your dog's name for the collar tag, for example Biscuit.
3. Optional: type your colours, for example Body: Grey, Accent: Desert Tan, Collar: Mandarin Orange, Tag: Cream, Lettering: Red. Leave it empty to get the colours in the photos.

Names of up to 8 letters look best, because the letters get smaller as the name gets longer. I check that your name fits and that the colours are easy to read together. If something does not work, I message you before I print.

### Great for

- Dachshund and sausage dog lovers
- Desks, craft tables, dressing tables and hallways
- A new dog, a birthday or a housewarming
- Pens, make-up brushes, keys and small things that need a home

### Care

PLA plastic softens in heat, so keep it away from radiators and sunny windows. It is for indoor use. Wipe it with a damp cloth. It is not a toy and it has small parts, so keep it away from young children. A phone thicker than about 13 mm with its case will not fit the slot.

Need another colour or a different number of compartments? Send me a message before you order.
