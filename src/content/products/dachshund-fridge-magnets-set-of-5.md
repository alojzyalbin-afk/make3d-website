A set of five dachshund fridge magnets, all in the same colour. Each one is the long, low outline of a sausage dog, with a white eye, an engraved ear line and, if you want one, a name in raised white letters.

Stick them on the fridge, a whiteboard, a filing cabinet or a locker. Keep the whole set for yourself or share it out with other dachshund fans.

### Details

- Set of 5 magnets, all the same colour
- Size of each magnet: approx. 75 x 35 mm and 4 mm thick
- Coloured body with a white eye, an engraved ear line and an optional name in raised white letters
- Two small magnets set into the back of each piece
- PLA plastic, printed in two colours. The colour comes from the plastic itself, with no paint
- 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland

### Colours

Choose one in the order form: Apple Green, Mint Green, Olive Green, Red, Orange, Blue, Ice Blue, Cherry Red, Pink or Mustard Green. All five magnets in your set come in that colour. Shades can differ a little from your screen to real life.

The name and the eye are always white. On the paler colours (Ice Blue, Mint Green, Apple Green, Mustard Green and Pink) the white name has less contrast, so it looks softer.

### How to personalise

Type a name into the order form if you want one. The same name is printed on all five magnets. Short names look best, up to about 8 letters. Leave the box empty and you get five plain dachshunds with no name.

### Great for

- Dachshund owners and sausage dog fans
- Fridges, whiteboards, lockers and filing cabinets
- Party favours and stocking fillers

### Care

PLA plastic softens in heat, so keep the magnets away from radiators, cookers, sunny windows and hot cars. Wipe them with a damp cloth.

Not a toy: each piece has two small magnets set into the back, so keep the set away from young children.
