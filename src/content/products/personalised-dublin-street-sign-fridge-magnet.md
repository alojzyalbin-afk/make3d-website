A personalised fridge magnet styled like the blue and white street signs of Dublin. Each one shows the Irish (Gaeilge) and English names of the place you choose, plus your own number.



Celebrate a new home in Dublin, remember your roots in Crumlin or give someone a keepsake that is nothing like a generic souvenir.



DETAILS

\- Size: approx. 80 x 17 mm and 3.5 mm thick

\- Matte PLA plastic in Dublin blue and white

\- Strong magnets on the back, ready for the fridge or any magnetic surface

\- Designed and 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland



HOW TO PERSONALISE

Type your details into the personalisation box:

1. Street, suburb or name in English (for example Crumlin, James's Street, Citywest or a person's name)

2. The number for the blue square (for example 12, 8 or 24)

3. Optional: your own Irish text for the top line. If you leave it out, I add the traditional Irish translation for you.

\- Going-away and diaspora gifts (a piece of home wherever you live)

\- Souvenirs for visitors



DELIVERY

Made to order in Dublin, Ireland. Ships from Ireland, so EU buyers pay no customs fees.



BEFORE YOU BUY

Every piece is 3D printed to order in Dublin. Because it is built up layer by layer in plastic, please know what to expect:

\- Fine layer lines are part of the look. The surface is not smooth like moulded plastic. You may see or feel the lines where each layer was printed.

\- The side that was printed against the build plate has a fine texture from the textured PEI plate I use.

\- Small seam marks can appear where each layer starts and ends. They look like a thin line or tiny bumps.

\- Small marks or tiny spots can appear, especially where one colour meets another. They come from the printing process and cannot be wiped off.

\- Colours can look a little different from the photos, depending on your screen and the light.

These are normal for 3D printing and do not affect how the item works. Because each piece is made to order, no two are exactly alike.

If you have any questions before you buy, send me a message and I will be happy to help.
