A set of five dachshund keyrings, all in the same colour. Each keyring is the long, low outline of a sausage dog with a white eye, an engraved ear line and, if you want one, your name in raised white letters.

Clip them on your house keys, a bag, a lanyard or a zip pull. Keep the whole set for yourself or hand them out to other dachshund fans.

### Details

- Set of 5 keyrings, all the same colour
- Size of each keyring: approx. 75 x 35 mm and 4 mm thick
- Coloured body with a white eye, an engraved ear line and an optional name in raised white letters
- Small loop above the dog's back with a hanging hole, ready for a split ring or a lanyard clip. Split rings are not included
- PLA plastic, printed in two colours. The colour comes from the plastic itself, with no paint
- 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland

### Colours

Choose one in the order form: Apple Green, Mint Green, Olive Green, Red, Orange, Blue, Ice Blue, Cherry Red, Pink or Mustard Green. All five keyrings in your set come in that colour. Shades can differ a little from your screen to real life.

The name and the eye are always white. On the paler colours (Ice Blue, Mint Green, Apple Green, Mustard Green and Pink) the white name has less contrast, so it looks softer.

### How to personalise

Type a name into the order form if you want one. The same name is printed on all five keyrings. Short names look best, up to about 8 letters. Leave the box empty and you get five plain dachshunds with no name.

### Great for

- Dachshund owners and sausage dog fans
- House keys, car keys, backpacks and lanyards
- New dog owners, dog walkers and dog sitters
- Party bags, thank-you gifts and small prizes

### Care

PLA plastic softens in heat, so keep the keyrings away from radiators, sunny windows and hot cars. Wipe them with a damp cloth. The tail is thin, so do not bend it or pull on it.

Not a toy: keep the set away from young children.
