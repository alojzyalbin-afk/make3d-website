A miniature barber pole lamp inspired by the classic signs outside traditional barbershops. It gives a warm, retro glow on a desk, a shelf or a reception counter. It is an accent light rather than a lamp for lighting a whole room.

The colours are classic red, royal blue and cream white, in a matte finish that avoids the shiny plastic look.

### Details

- Height: 23 cm (about 9 inches)
- Diameter: 9.2 cm (about 3.6 inches), a compact round footprint
- Matte PLA plastic
- Comes with an attached USB power cable
- Made to order in my Dublin workshop

### Power

The cable plugs into any standard USB port, phone charger or power bank. A USB wall adapter is not included.

### Perfect for

- Barbershop, salon and hairdresser decor for the cutting station, reception desk or waiting area
- Gifts for barbers and hairstylists: graduation, a new shop opening or a birthday
- Man caves and retro desks, for anyone who loves a classic barbershop look
