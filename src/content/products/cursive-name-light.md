A personalised name light: a large letter with your name in flowing cursive, lit softly from inside. Made to order for a nursery, a child's bedroom, a desk or a gift table.

The letter and the name are 3D printed and assembled by me in Dublin. Choose the size that suits the space and send me the name you want.

### Details

- Sizes from 150 mm to 250 mm tall (pick your size in the order form)
- Cursive name set into a large letter, lit by built-in LEDs
- Powered by USB-C at 5 V and flameless: no batteries and no open flame
- The colour comes from the plastic itself, with no paint
- You choose the colour of the letter and the colour of the name
- Designed and 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland

### Great for

- Nursery decor and baby shower gifts
- New baby and christening gifts
- Kids' bedrooms and playrooms
- Desk lamps and gamer tag lights
- Birthday and Christmas presents

### How to personalise

1. Choose your size in the order form.
2. Fill in these details in the order form: the main letter, the name for the cursive line, the letter colour, the name colour and any notes.

I confirm everything with you before it is printed. If you are not sure about the spelling, the style or which size suits your name, send me a message and I will help you choose.

### Power

The light runs from any standard USB port, phone charger or power bank with a USB-C cable.
