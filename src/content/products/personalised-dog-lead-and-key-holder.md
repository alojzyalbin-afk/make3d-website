A personalised wall holder for your keys and your dog's lead, with your family name on the front and an optional pet name under a paw print. Hang it by the front door and the keys and the lead always have a place to go.

You choose how many hooks it has, the colour of the plate, the colour of the lettering and the colour of the hooks. I set up the layout around your names and print it to order.

### Details

- Size: approx. 175 mm wide x 70 mm high and 6 mm thick with 4 hooks
- More hooks means a wider plate: each extra hook adds about 35 mm. 5 hooks is approx. 210 mm wide and 6 hooks approx. 245 mm wide
- Your family name in raised lettering, on one line or on two lines, for example The Byrnes or Byrne / Family
- Optional pet name under a raised paw print. Leave it out and the family name gets more room
- Plate and lettering in PLA plastic, the colour comes from the plastic itself with no paint
- Hooks in PETG plastic, printed separately and fitted into slots in the plate
- Hangs on two nails or screws, using the hanging slots on the back
- Designed and 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland

### What to hang on it

Made for keys on a ring and a dog lead or a cat harness. The paw print suits a cat as well as a dog. It is meant for light everyday items, not heavy coats or bags.

### Colours

Plate and lettering (PLA, pick one colour for each): White, Black, Grey, Navy Blue, Blue, Sky Blue, Mint Green, Grass Green, Mustard Green, Yellow, Mandarin Orange, Red, Pink, Desert Tan, Latte Brown, Brown or Gold

Hooks (PETG): White, Blue, Black or Red

Shown in the photos: mint green plate, white lettering and white hooks.

Popular combinations (plate and lettering): Mint Green and White, Navy Blue and White, Black and White, White and Navy Blue, Desert Tan and Brown, Mandarin Orange and White

### How to order

1. Choose the number of hooks and the hook colour in the order form.
2. In the first personalisation box, type your family name and, if you want one, your pet's name. Use a / to start a second line. Example: Byrne / Family and Pet: Biscuit.
3. In the colours box, type the colour of the plate and the colour of the lettering, for example Plate: Mint Green, Text: White.

I check that your text fits and that the two colours are easy to read together. If something does not work, I message you before I print.

### Great for

- Housewarming and new home gifts
- New puppy and new dog owner gifts
- Hallways, porches and utility rooms

### Care

PLA plastic softens in heat, so keep it away from radiators. It is for indoor use. Wipe it with a damp cloth.

Need a longer name, two pets or a different layout? Send me a message before you order.
