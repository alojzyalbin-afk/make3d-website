A personalised business card holder with a 3D printed tooth on the front and your name, title or clinic name in raised letters. It holds your cards on the reception desk or the office desk and gives patients something to talk about.

It also works as a thank-you gift for a dentist, orthodontist or hygienist. It suits a graduation present for a newly qualified dental student too.

### Details

- Matte white 3D tooth next to a name plate with raised, two-colour text
- Default colours: medical blue base, white text and a white tooth
- A slot that holds standard business cards
- Flat base that sits steadily on a wooden or glass desk
- Matte PLA plastic, designed and 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland
- Size: approx. 200mm x 63mm x 100mm

### Other professions

Want this style for another job? I can swap the tooth for a simple symbol such as a stethoscope for a doctor, the scales of justice for a lawyer or a house for an estate agent. Please message me before you order so we can agree the design.

### How to personalise

Type the text into the order form exactly as you want it shown (name, title or clinic name, for example Dr. Sarah Murphy).

I lay out the text for you, print it fresh and send it from Dublin.
