A personalised name light: a large letter with your name in flowing cursive, lit softly from inside. Made to order for a nursery, a child's bedroom, a desk or a gift table.



The letter and the name are 3D printed and assembled by me in Dublin. Choose the size that suits the space and send me the name you want.



DETAILS

\- Sizes from 150 mm to 250 mm tall (pick your size in the drop-down)

\- Cursive name set into a large letter, lit by built-in LEDs

\- Powered by USB-C at 5 V and flameless: no batteries and no open flame

\- The colour comes from the plastic itself, with no paint

\- You choose the colour of the letter and the colour of the name

\- Designed and 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland



GREAT FOR

\- Nursery decor and baby shower gifts

\- New baby and christening gifts

\- Kids' bedrooms and playrooms

\- Desk lamps and gamer tag lights

\- Birthday and Christmas presents



HOW TO PERSONALISE

1. Choose your size in the drop-down menu.

2. Type these details into the personalisation box: the main letter, the name for the cursive line, the letter colour, the name colour and any notes.

I confirm everything with you before it is printed. If you are not sure about the spelling, the style or which size suits your name, send me a message and I will help you choose.



DELIVERY

Made to order in Dublin, Ireland. Ships from Ireland, so EU buyers pay no customs fees.



POWER

The light runs from any standard USB port, phone charger or power bank with a USB-C cable.



BEFORE YOU BUY

Every piece is 3D printed to order in Dublin. Because it is built up layer by layer in plastic, please know what to expect:

\- Fine layer lines are part of the look. The surface is not smooth like moulded plastic. You may see or feel the lines where each layer was printed.

\- The side that was printed against the build plate has a fine texture from the textured PEI plate I use.

\- Small seam marks can appear where each layer starts and ends. They look like a thin line or tiny bumps.

\- Small marks or tiny spots can appear, especially where one colour meets another. They come from the printing process and cannot be wiped off.

\- Colours can look a little different from the photos, depending on your screen and the light.

These are normal for 3D printing and do not affect how the item works. Because each piece is made to order, no two are exactly alike.

If you have any questions before you buy, send me a message and I will be happy to help.



International orders (USA, Canada, Australia and other non-EU countries): import duties, taxes and carrier fees are NOT included in the price or shipping. They are charged by the destination country and paid by the buyer before delivery. I will message you an estimate before I ship and will only post your order once you agree.
