A personalised fridge magnet in the shape of a Dublin Georgian door, with the red brick, the white surround, the fanlight and the panelled door you see on streets all over the city. Your family name goes on the sign above the door, your house number goes on the door and you choose the colour of the door.

Put your own front door on the fridge, or send a small piece of Dublin to someone who has moved away.

### Details

- Size: 80 mm x 50 mm and 5.5 mm thick
- Red brick wall with a raised brick pattern, white door surround with a fanlight and a white name sign with raised black lettering
- Panelled door with your house number, a letterbox and a door knocker in silver, as in the photos
- Four small magnets set into the back
- PLA plastic, the colour comes from the plastic itself with no paint
- Designed and 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland

### Door colour

Choose Green, Blue, Red, Yellow, Black or Grey. Want a different colour? Type it in the door colour box, for example Purple. The brick, the white surround and the black lettering stay as in the photos.

### How to order

1. Type the name for the sign above the door and your house number, written exactly as you want them, for example The Byrnes and 27.
2. Type the door colour.

Short names look best. A very long name gets smaller letters, so send me a message first if yours is over 12 characters. House numbers with 1 or 2 digits fit best, so send me a message first if yours has 3 digits or a letter, like 27A. I check that everything fits before I print.

### Great for

- Housewarming and new home presents
- Irish people living abroad and anyone missing home
- Anyone who loves the Georgian doors of Dublin
- Stocking fillers and Secret Santa gifts

### Care

PLA plastic softens in heat, so keep it away from radiators and cookers. It is for indoor use. Wipe it with a damp cloth. Not a toy: it contains small magnets, so keep it away from young children.

Questions about colours, names or numbers? Send me a message before you order.
