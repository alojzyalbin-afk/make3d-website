A lamp shaped like an open straight razor, standing on its own round base, with your name on the glowing blade and a second text on the tail of the razor. Everything you see is 3D printed in plastic. There is no metal in it except a few small screws and nuts.

Switch it on and the blade lights up from the inside, with your name showing dark against the glow. It sits well on a barber shop counter, a reception desk, a shelf or a bedside table. It is a unique design. I drew it from scratch and I could not find another lamp like it anywhere. I print each one to order in Dublin.

### Details

- Straight razor lamp on a round base, with an arm that holds the razor open
- Your name on the blade: dark letters against the glowing blade when the lamp is on and soft grey letters when it is off
- Your own text on the tang (the tail of the razor), on up to two short lines, in small raised letters. For example Barber / Shop
- Size: approx. 331 mm tall x 218 mm wide x 101 mm deep (about 33 x 22 x 10 cm). Round base 100 mm across. Blade 135 mm long and 38 mm wide. All sizes are approximate, within about 1 mm
- Stands on its own, no fixing needed
- Colours: black, cream white and gold. The colour comes from the plastic itself, with no paint. The gold is plastic, not metal
- PLA plastic
- Built-in LED strip for the light
- USB cable 1m long with an on/off switch on the cable. It plugs into a phone charger (not included)
- Designed and 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland

### What is printed and what is not

- Printed in plastic: the base, the arm, the razor handle, the blade and the lettering
- Metal: only a few small screws and nuts that hold the parts together. No metal razor, no metal base, no metal arm
- Not printed: the LED light and the USB cable with its switch
- The blade is plastic and has no cutting edge. This is a lamp, not a razor

### Colour

This lamp is made in one colour combination only: black, cream white and gold, as in the photos. Want other colours? Send me a message before you order and I will tell you if I can do it. Please write first, so nothing is printed in the wrong colours.

### How to personalise

1. Type your name or your shop name for the blade, for example Joe's Barbers. It prints on the blade exactly as you type it, with capitals and lowercase letters.
2. Type the text for the tang, on up to two short lines. Use a / to start a new line, for example Barber / Shop.

Short texts look best. Both lines on the tang are printed at the same size, so one long line makes both smaller. I check that your text fits and looks right before I print. If it would come out cramped or cannot be done, I message you first.

### Care

For indoor use only. PLA plastic softens in heat, so keep it away from radiators and sunny windows. Keep it dry. Unplug it before you clean it and wipe it with a dry cloth. It is not a toy.
