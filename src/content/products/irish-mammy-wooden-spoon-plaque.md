A funny wall plaque for the Irish kitchen: "Irish Mammy's Wooden Spoon of Discipline", with the wooden spoon included. The spoon rests in its own holder on the front, where everyone can see it. Consider it a friendly warning.

The plaque is shield-shaped, with a Celtic triquetra knot at the top and four lines of raised lettering. The spoon holder sits at the bottom. It hangs flat on the wall and no hook or fixing shows on the front.

### Details

- Size: 22.5 cm x 17 cm x 28 cm (width x height x depth, depth measured at the spoon holder)
- Wooden spoon included
- Raised border, Celtic knot and lettering: Irish Mammy's / Wooden Spoon / of / Discipline
- Plaque and holder in PLA plastic, the colour comes from the plastic itself with no paint
- Hangs on two nails or screws (not included), using two hidden keyhole slots on the back. The slots are 10.5 cm apart
- Designed and 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland

### Colours

Shown in the photos: pale yellow face and holder, taupe border, knot and lettering.

You can change them. Pick one colour for the face and the holder and one for the border, the knot and the lettering: White, Black, Grey, Navy Blue, Blue, Sky Blue, Mint Green, Grass Green, Mustard Green, Yellow, Mandarin Orange, Red, Pink, Desert Tan, Latte Brown, Brown or Gold

Write your choice in the order form, for example Face and holder: Mint Green, Border and text: White. Leave it empty and I print it in the colours shown in the photos.

Nothing to choose from a list, because the price is the same in every colour. If you want other colours, use the order form. I check that the two colours are easy to read together. If something does not work, I message you before I print.

### Care

PLA plastic softens in heat, so keep the plaque away from the cooker, radiators and sunny windows. It is for indoor use. Wipe it with a damp cloth. Do not soak the wooden spoon or put it in the dishwasher.

The spoon is a warning, not a weapon. It stays on the wall.
