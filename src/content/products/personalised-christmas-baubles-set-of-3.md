A set of 3 personalised Christmas baubles, each with a name in raised letters. You choose the shape and the name for every bauble, plus one pair of colours for the whole set. Printed to order in Dublin, ready to hang on the tree, in a window or on a gift.

### The 5 shapes

Pick a shape for each of the 3 baubles:

1. **Paw:** a paw print with a Christmas tree and a star, made for a pet's name
2. **Mountain:** mountain peaks above a row of pine trees
3. **Reindeer:** a reindeer between two pine trees under the name, with a leafy sprig and a star
4. **Bow:** a bow under the hanging loop, with a pine sprig, a poinsettia, a heart and a star
5. **Bell:** a bell on the left with pine branches, snowflakes and two pine trees

### Details

- Set of 3 baubles, 3D printed in PLA plastic
- Round baubles are 90 mm across. The paw is 95 mm wide and 86 mm high
- 2 mm thick, with the name raised 1 mm on top
- Each bauble has a hanging loop with a hole
- Printed in two colours. The colours come from the plastic itself, with no paint
- Made by me in Dublin, Ireland

### Colours

All 3 baubles in a set share the same two colours: one for the bauble and one for the name. Choose both in the order form from the two ranges below, and write the range with the colour, for example Matte Red or Simple Red.

- **Matte range:** White, Grey, Black, Latte Brown, Dark Brown, Tan, Red, Orange, Yellow, Apple Green, Mint Green, Grass Green, Dark Green, Ice Blue, Marine Blue, Navy Blue, Purple and Pink
- **Simple range:** White, Cream White, Grey, Black, Cherry Red, Red, Pink, Sunny Orange, Pumpkin Orange, Apple Green, Mint Green, Olive Green, Mustard Green, Ice Blue, Soft Blue and Cobalt Blue

Shades can differ a little from your screen to real life. Please pick two colours that look clearly different, so the name is easy to read. If they are the same or too close, I will message you before printing so we can change one.

### How to personalise

In the order form write the shape and the name for each of the 3 baubles, then the bauble colour and the name colour.

For example: Bauble 1 Reindeer - Marius, Bauble 2 Bow - Valentina, Bauble 3 Paw - Lola, bauble colour Simple Red, name colour Simple White.

Names are printed exactly as you type them, so please check the spelling. Names of up to 10 letters fit well. Longer name? Send me a message before you order.

### Great for

- Your own tree, with a bauble for each member of the family
- Pet lovers: a paw with the name of your dog or cat
- Secret Santa and stocking fillers
- Teacher and colleague gifts

### Care

PLA plastic softens in heat, so keep the baubles away from radiators, candles and hot bulbs. Wipe them with a dry or damp cloth.

Not a toy: keep them away from young children.
