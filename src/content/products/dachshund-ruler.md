A sausage dog ruler with a 15 cm scale along its long, straight back. The 0 sits right where the neck ends, so you can measure and draw straight lines from the very first mark.

It is a fun, useful piece for the desk, the craft table or the sewing box. It also brings a small smile to anyone who loves dachshunds.

### Details

- Size: approx. 290 x 136 mm and 3 mm thick
- 15 cm scale with millimetre, half centimetre and centimetre marks, numbered 0 to 15
- Raised white marks, numbers and eye on a red dog, with an engraved ear line
- PLA plastic, printed in two colours. The colour comes from the plastic itself, with no paint
- The marks follow a 1 mm scale, but this is a 3D printed novelty ruler, not a certified measuring tool
- 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland

### Custom colours

Want the dog in black, brown or tan to match your own dachshund? Send me a message before you order and I will tell you which colours I can do.

### Great for

- Dachshund owners and sausage dog fans
- Desks, home offices, craft tables and sewing boxes
- Teachers, students and anyone who still draws by hand
- Stocking fillers and Secret Santa

### Care

PLA plastic softens in heat, so keep it away from radiators, sunny windows and hot cars. Wipe it with a damp cloth. It is a ruler, not a chew toy, so keep it away from your dog.
