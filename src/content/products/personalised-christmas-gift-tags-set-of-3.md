A set of 3 personalised Christmas gift tags, each with a raised border, a festive design and a name in raised letters. You choose the designs, the colours and the names. Printed to order in Dublin, ready to tie to a present or hang on the tree, on a stocking or in a window.

### The 5 designs

Choose any combination of 3: all the same, two the same with one different or all different.

- Pine Tree - a pine tree standing along the left edge
- Gift Bow - a big bow on a ribbon that runs down the tag, like a wrapped present
- Santa Hat - a Santa hat in the bottom left corner
- Snowflake - a large snowflake along the left edge
- Holly - holly leaves and berries in the top right corner

### Details

- Set of 3 tags, 3D printed in PLA plastic
- Each tag is 50 mm wide and 96 mm high
- 3 mm thick in total: the base is 2 mm and the raised border, design and name are 1 mm
- Each tag has a hanging hole of 5 mm
- Printed in two colours. The colours come from the plastic itself, with no paint
- 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland

### Colours

Pick one colour combination for the whole set: one colour for the base and one for the border, the design and the name. All 3 tags share the same colours. Only the designs and the names can be different. Shades can differ a little from the photos and from your screen to real life.

### How to personalise

Write the design and the name for each tag in the order form, in the same order.

Example: Tag 1: Santa Hat - Valentina. Tag 2: Gift Bow - Marius. Tag 3: Pine Tree - Lola.

The name goes in the free space of each design, in raised letters. Names are printed exactly as you type them, so please check the spelling. Short names look best. A longer name? Send me a message before you order.

### Great for

- Labelling presents with a tag that can hang on the tree afterwards
- A family or a group of friends, one tag each
- Pet lovers: the name of your dog or cat
- Secret Santa and stocking fillers
- Teacher and colleague gifts

### Care

PLA plastic softens in heat, so keep the tags away from radiators, candles and hot bulbs. Wipe with a dry or damp cloth. Not a toy: keep them away from young children.

### Before you buy

Every piece is 3D printed to order in Dublin, built up layer by layer in plastic. Fine layer lines are part of the look, the side printed against the plate has a fine texture and small seam marks or tiny spots can appear where one colour meets another. These are normal for 3D printing and do not affect how the tag works. Because each set is made to order, no two are exactly alike.
