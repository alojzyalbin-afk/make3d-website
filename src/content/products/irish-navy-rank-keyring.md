A personalised keyring shaped like a navy epaulette, with your rank on the front and your surname in raised white letters along the bottom. It is made for sailors of the Irish Naval Service, serving or retired. It also suits the family and friends who want to give them something that is not on a shop shelf.

Choose your rank in the order form, tell me your surname and I design and print it for you.

### Details

- Epaulette-shaped keyring with the rank insignia and your surname in raised white lettering
- Navy blue body with white raised details, printed layer by layer in two colours
- Matte PLA plastic
- Gold-coloured metal chain and split ring included
- Designed and 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland
- Size: approx. 65 x 35 mm and 3.7 mm thick

### Ranks available (Irish Naval Service enlisted ranks, OR-1 to OR-9)

- Recruit (no insignia)
- Ordinary Seaman
- Able Seaman
- Leading Seaman
- Petty Officer
- Senior Petty Officer
- Chief Petty Officer
- Senior Chief Petty Officer
- Warrant Officer

### How to personalise

1. Choose your rank in the order form.
2. Type your surname into the order form. It prints in capital letters and works best at 10 letters or fewer, like MURPHY.

If your name is longer, message me and I will suggest the best fit before I print.

### Great for

- Promotion and retirement keepsakes
- A gift from the family and friends of a sailor
- Locker keys, kit bags and car keys

### Important

This is an independent, handmade keyring. It is not an official product of the Irish Defence Forces or the Irish Naval Service and it is not endorsed by them. It is a keyring, not a uniform item. It is not made to be worn as rank insignia.

Need an Air Corps rank, a different colour or an order for a whole crew? Send me a message before you order. Looking for an Army rank? I make those too.
