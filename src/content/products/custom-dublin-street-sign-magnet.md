Bring a piece of Dublin's iconic streets straight to your fridge!

Whether you want to celebrate your new home in Dublin, remember your roots in Crumlin or surprise a loved one with a unique Irish keepsake, this custom miniature Dublin street sign is the perfect choice. Styled exactly like the famous blue and white signs found across Ireland's capital, each piece features both the Irish (Gaeilge) and English names of your favorite location.

✨ Perfect For:

\- Housewarming Gifts: A thoughtful and personal touch for anyone moving into a new home in Ireland.

\- Going Away / Diaspora Gifts: Keep a piece of home close, no matter where in the world you are.

\- Memorable Souvenirs: Move past the generic plastic trinkets with a premium, custom-made Irish token.

📐 Product Details & Dimensions:

\- Size: Approximately 140mm x 35mm x 6mm (Perfect fridge magnet size).

\- Finish: Made using premium matte and silk materials for a clean, professional look with rich colors (Dublin Blue and Crisp White). No cheap glossy plastic look!

Mounting: Equipped with strong magnets on the back, ready to snap onto any magnetic surface.

✍️ How to Personalize:

\- Enter your chosen Street or Suburb Name (e.g., Crumlin, James's Street, Citywest) or maybe a person's name.

\- Enter your preferred Zone or House Number (e.g., 12, 8, 24).

Note: We will automatically include the correct traditional Irish translation (Gaeilge) on top! If you have a custom text request for the top line instead, just let us know in the notes.
