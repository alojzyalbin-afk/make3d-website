A set of 3 personalised Christmas name tags, each with a festive design and a name box with the name in raised letters. You choose the designs, the colours and the names. Printed to order in Dublin, ready to tie to a present or hang on the Christmas tree, on a stocking or in a window.

### The 4 designs

Choose any combination of 3: all the same, two the same with one different or all different.

- Gingerbread Man - a smiling gingerbread man with a bow tie, hearts and stars
- Reindeer - a reindeer in a scarf, with snowflakes
- Gnome - a gnome in a tall hat, holding a present
- Christmas Tree - a tree with a star, baubles and two presents under it

### Details

- Set of 3 tags, 3D printed in PLA plastic
- Each tag is 50 mm wide and 96 mm high
- 3 mm thick in total. The name is raised another 1 mm on top of the name box, so the tag is 4 mm thick at the name
- The name box at the bottom of each tag is 44 x 16 mm
- Each tag has a hanging hole of 5 mm
- The colours come from the plastic itself, with no paint
- 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland

### Colours

Pick one colour combination for the whole set. All 3 tags share the same colours. Only the designs and the names can be different. Shades can differ a little from the photos and from your screen to real life.

### How to personalise

Write the design and the name for each tag in the order form, in the same order.

Example: Tag 1: Gnome - Lola. Tag 2: Gingerbread Man - Marius. Tag 3: Reindeer - Valentina.

Names are printed exactly as you type them, so please check the spelling. Names of up to 10 letters fit well. A longer name? Send me a message before you order.

### Great for

- Stocking tags with the name of everyone in the family
- Labelling presents with a tag that can hang on the tree afterwards
- Pet lovers: the name of your dog or cat
- Secret Santa and stocking fillers
- Teacher and colleague gifts

### Care

PLA plastic softens in heat, so keep the tags away from radiators, candles and hot bulbs. Wipe with a dry or damp cloth. Not a toy: keep them away from young children.

### Before you buy

Every piece is 3D printed to order in Dublin, built up layer by layer in plastic. Fine layer lines are part of the look, the side printed against the plate has a fine texture and small seam marks or tiny spots can appear where one colour meets another. These are normal for 3D printing and do not affect how the tag works. Because each set is made to order, no two are exactly alike.
