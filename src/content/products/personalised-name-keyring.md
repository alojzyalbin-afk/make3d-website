A keyring with your own name in raised, rounded bubble letters, printed in two colours that you choose. Pick one colour for the base and one for the letters, type the name and I print it to order in Dublin.

Clip it on your house keys, a bag, a backpack or a lanyard.

### Details

- Your name in raised letters on a coloured base: 2 mm base plus 2 mm letters, so 4 mm thick in total
- Height approx. 21 mm. The length depends on the name: short names make a short keyring and longer names a longer one
- Metal split ring and chain included
- PLA plastic printed in two colours. The colours come from the plastic itself, with no paint
- 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland

### Colours

Choose a base colour and a letter colour in the order form. Both lists offer the same 16 colours: White, Cream White, Grey, Black, Cherry Red, Red, Pink, Sunny Orange, Pumpkin Orange, Apple Green, Mint Green, Olive Green, Mustard Green, Ice Blue, Soft Blue and Cobalt Blue. Shades can differ a little from your screen to real life.

Please pick two colours that look clearly different, so the name is easy to read. If your two colours are the same or too close, I will message you before printing so we can change one.

### How to personalise

Type the name in the order form, up to 15 characters (spaces count). It is printed exactly as you type it, so please check the spelling.

Names longer than 15 characters: send me a message before you order and we will see what fits.

### Great for

- Your own keys, bag or backpack
- Birthday and thank-you gifts
- Stocking fillers
- Teacher and colleague gifts

### Care

PLA plastic softens in heat, so keep the keyring away from radiators, sunny windows and hot cars. Wipe it with a damp cloth. Keys and coins scratch any plastic, so a keyring that lives in a pocket or a bag will show some wear over time.

Not a toy: the split ring and chain are small parts, so keep it away from young children.
