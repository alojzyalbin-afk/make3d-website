A dignified, handcrafted way to keep your beloved companion close. This pyramid-shaped memorial urn is designed and 3D printed in matte black PLA with warm gold accents, finished with a personalized engraved plaque.

The urn opens by unscrewing the top from the base, so you can place your pet's ashes directly inside, or use your own keepsake container if you prefer (the internal cavity fits most small containers). The price includes the urn either way.

✔ Fully personalized plaque: name and dates of your choosing

✔ Heart symbol shown, but can be swapped for a paw print or another simple shape on request

✔ Threaded lid, no glue needed, opens and closes securely

✔ Base 134 x 134 mm, height approx. 135 mm

✔ Matte black PLA body with gold PLA+ accents

How to order:

Leave a note at checkout (or comment/DM me) with:

Pet's name

Dates (birth - passing, or however you'd like it shown)

Symbol choice: heart (default), paw print, or describe another simple shape

Any color preference for the plaque (default gold)

Made with care in Ireland. Please allow a little extra time for this piece, as each one is finished individually.

This listing is for the physical urn. It does not include cremation services.
