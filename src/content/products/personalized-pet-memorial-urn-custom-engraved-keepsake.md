A dignified, handmade way to keep your companion close. This pyramid-shaped memorial urn is designed and 3D printed in matte black PLA with warm gold accents. A personalised plaque carries your pet's name and dates.

The top unscrews from the base, so you can place your pet's ashes straight inside or use your own small keepsake container. The inner space fits most small containers. The price includes the urn in either case.

### Details

- Base: 134 x 134 mm. Height: approx. 135 mm
- Interior volume: approx 0.25L (254 cm³)
- Matte black PLA body with gold PLA+ accents
- Threaded lid: no glue needed, it opens and closes securely
- Personalised plaque with your pet's name and dates, printed in raised lettering
- A heart symbol is shown. I can swap it for a paw print or another simple shape on request
- Made with care in Ireland. Each piece is finished individually, so please allow a little extra time.

### How to personalise

Fill in these details in the order form:

1. Pet's name
2. Dates (birth and passing, in any format you like)
3. Symbol: heart (default), paw print or another simple shape you describe
4. Plaque colour if you want a change (the default is gold)

Please check that the inside space suits your pet before you order. If you are unsure, send me a message and I will help.

This is for the physical urn. It does not include cremation services.
