A little pumpkin house lantern for autumn. Put a flameless LED tea light inside and it sends a warm glow through the tiny windows and the open door. Inspired by woodland fairy tales and cottagecore style.

### The tea light is not included

This is the lantern only. You need to add your own battery-operated LED tea light.

Each house is printed in separate parts so the colours stay rich: an orange pumpkin base, a teal door, a brown roof and small coloured leaves. I assemble every one by hand in my Dublin workshop.

### Details

- Lantern only: the LED tea light is NOT included
- Hollow inside, made for a battery-operated LED tea light
- Matte PLA plastic in autumn colours, with no shiny plastic look
- Assembled by hand from separately printed parts
- Designed and 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland

### Important

Use flameless LED tea lights only. Never use a real candle inside.

### Perfect for

- Halloween and autumn table decor, mantelpieces and windowsills
- Thanksgiving centrepieces and seasonal displays
- Housewarming and birthday gifts for anyone who loves a cosy autumn home

Want the roof in another colour or a different mix of leaves? Send me a message before you order and I will adjust the colours to suit your room.
