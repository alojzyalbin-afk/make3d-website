A cosy little gnome cottage lantern. It is shaped like a classic Santa hat, with little pine trees and a candy cane on its base. Put a flameless LED tea light inside and the windows and the door glow.

### The tea light is not included

This is the lantern only. You need to add your own flameless battery-operated LED tea light. Place it inside, switch it on and the windows light up. No open flame, ever.

### Details

- Lantern only: the LED tea light is NOT included
- Height: approx. 120 mm
- Width of the hat section: approx. 70 mm
- 3D printed in Ireland

### Perfect for

- A shelf, mantelpiece or windowsill at Christmas
- Stocking fillers and Secret Santa gifts
- A cosy festive glow on a bedroom shelf, with your own LED tea light

### Safety note

Use a flameless LED tea light only. Never put a real candle inside. This is not a toy, so please keep it away from young children.
