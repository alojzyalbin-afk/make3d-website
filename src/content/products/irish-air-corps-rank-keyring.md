A personalised keyring shaped like an epaulette, with your Irish Air Corps rank on the front and your surname in raised white letters along the bottom. It is made for members of the Irish Air Corps, serving or retired. It also suits the family and friends who want to give them something that is not on a shop shelf.

Choose your rank in the order form, tell me your surname and I design and print it for you.

### Details

- Epaulette-shaped keyring with the rank insignia, wings and your surname in raised white lettering
- Bright blue body with a light grey frame and white raised details, printed layer by layer in three colours
- Matte PLA plastic
- Silver-coloured metal chain and split ring included
- Designed and 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland
- Size: approx. 65 x 35 mm and 3.7 mm thick

### Ranks available (Irish Air Corps enlisted ranks, OR-1 to OR-9)

- Apprentice
- 2 Star Airman
- 3 Star Airman
- Corporal
- Sergeant
- Flight Quartermaster Sergeant
- Flight Sergeant
- Regimental Quartermaster Sergeant
- Regimental Sergeant Major

### How to personalise

1. Choose your rank in the order form.
2. Type your surname into the order form. It prints in capital letters and works best at 10 letters or fewer, like MURPHY.

If your name is longer, message me and I will suggest the best fit before I print.

### Great for

- Promotion and retirement keepsakes
- A gift from the family and friends of someone serving in the Air Corps
- Locker keys, kit bags and car keys

### Important

This is an independent, handmade keyring. It is not an official product of the Irish Defence Forces or the Irish Air Corps and it is not endorsed by them. It is a keyring, not a uniform item. It is not made to be worn as rank insignia.

Need a different colour or an order for a whole squadron? Send me a message before you order. Looking for an Army or Naval Service rank? I make those too.
