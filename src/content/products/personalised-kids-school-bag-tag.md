### Your child's name

Type the name in the order form. It is printed in capital letters, exactly as you type it, so please check the spelling. Names of up to 8 letters fit on every design. A longer name or a nickname with spaces? Send me a message before you order.

### NFC chip (included)

Every tag has a small NFC chip sealed inside while it is printed. It cannot fall out and it does not show. The chip comes blank: I do not write anything on it and I never see or store your details. You programme it yourself in a few minutes with a free NFC app from your phone's app store. You decide what it holds, for example a phone number link.

- Android phones with NFC switched on usually read it
- iPhones from the XS onwards read it in the background. For this to work the chip must hold a link, such as a web address or a phone number link, not plain text
- Thick or metal phone cases can make reading harder

Please programme the chip and test it with your own phone and a few other phones before your child uses it. Put only what you are happy for a stranger to read. A parent's phone number is usually enough. I suggest not adding your child's address or school.

### What this is and is not

This is a printed ID tag with an NFC chip. It is not a toy, a tracker or a safety device and it does not replace supervision. I cannot promise that anyone will see it or scan it.

### Colours

Each tag has up to 3 colours: the base (the back and the outline), the middle (the main shapes) and the writing (the name, the words and any small details on top). If you write nothing about colours, your tag comes in the colours of that design in the photos.

Want other colours? Write them in the order form, for example: Base: Navy Blue / Middle: White / Writing: Red. Please pick colours that look clearly different from each other so the writing is easy to read. If your combination does not work, I will message you before printing. Shades can differ a little from the photos and your screen to real life.

### Care

PLA plastic softens in heat, so keep the tag away from radiators, sunny windows and hot cars. Wipe it with a damp cloth. Not a toy: an adult should attach it to the bag and check from time to time that it is still firmly attached.
