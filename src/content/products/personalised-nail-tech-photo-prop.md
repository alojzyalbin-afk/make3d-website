A personalised plaque made to be held in your hand while your freshly done nails are the star of the photo. It carries your name or business name in large raised lettering on up to three lines, inside a bow and wreath design, with finger notches around the edge so it sits comfortably in the hand.

Made for nail techs and nail artists who want branded photos for Instagram, TikTok and their portfolio. It also makes a keepsake for anyone who wants a photo of a fresh set.

Choose the colour of the plate, the border and the text, tell me what to print and I design and make it for you.

### Details

- Wreath-shaped plaque with a bow and finger notches along the edge
- Your text in large raised lettering, up to 3 lines
- You choose three colours: plate, border and text
- Size: approx. 103 mm wide x 140 mm high and 5 mm thick, small enough to hold in one hand
- PLA plastic, the colour comes from the plastic itself with no paint
- Designed and 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland
- The plaque only: the hand, nails and background in the photos are not included

### Colours available (pick one for each part)

Marble Blue, Ash Grey, Grass Green, Desert Tan, White, Dark Brown, Orange, Purple, Red, Green Matcha, Apple Green, Sakura Pink, Ice Blue, Yellow

Shown in the photos: purple plate, white border, red text.

### How to personalise

1. Type your text into the order form. About 16 characters fit on a line, for example Nails by Aoife on one line and Dublin on the next. Use a / to show where a new line starts.
2. Type your colours into the colours box: plate, border and text, for example Plate: Purple, Border: White, Text: Red.

I check that the text will be easy to read. If two colours are too close together, I will message you before I print.

### Great for

- Nail techs and nail artists, including mobile techs and students
- Client photos, portfolio shots and social media content
- A gift for a nail tech or for a new salon opening

### Care

PLA plastic softens in heat, so keep it away from radiators and hot cars. Wipe it with a damp cloth. It is a photo prop, not a tool for use on clients and it is not made to be sterilised.

Need a different colour, a longer name or an order for a whole team? Send me a message before you order.

Want a simple logo on your plaque? Send me a message before you order. I can only do simple, bold shapes, not detailed logos or photos.
