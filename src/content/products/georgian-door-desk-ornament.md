A personalised Georgian door ornament for your desk, shelf or windowsill. It is the Dublin door you see on streets all over the city, with the red brick, the white surround, the fanlight and the panelled door, standing upright on its own black base. Your family name goes on the sign above the door, your house number goes on the door and you choose the colour of the door.

Put your own front door on your desk, or send a small piece of Dublin to someone who has moved away.

### Details

- Size: approx. 110 x 185 mm
- Stands upright on its own black two-step base, no fixing needed
- Red brick wall with a raised brick pattern, white door surround with a fanlight and a white name sign with raised black lettering
- Panelled door with your house number, a ring door knocker, a letterbox and a door knob, all in white as in the photos
- PLA plastic, the colour comes from the plastic itself with no paint
- Designed and 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland

### Door colour

Choose Green, Blue, Red, Yellow, Black or Grey. Want a different colour? Type it in the door colour box, for example Purple. The brick, the white surround and the black lettering stay as in the photos.

### How to order

1. Type the name for the sign above the door and your house number, written exactly as you want them, for example The Byrnes and 26.
2. Type the door colour.

Names up to about 20 characters and house numbers of up to 3 characters, like 26, 126 or 26A, fit well. Send me a message first if yours are longer. I check that everything fits before I print.

### Great for

- Housewarming and new home presents
- Irish people living abroad and anyone missing home
- Anyone who loves the Georgian doors of Dublin
- A tidy touch for a desk, shelf or windowsill

### Care

PLA plastic softens in heat, so keep it away from radiators and cookers. It is for indoor use. Wipe it with a damp cloth. Keep it on a flat and stable surface, away from the edge.

Questions about colours, names or numbers? Send me a message before you order.
