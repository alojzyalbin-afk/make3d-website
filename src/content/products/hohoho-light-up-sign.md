A big HoHoHo sign that lights up from the inside. Three tall H letters alternate with three round Os dressed as Santa, an elf and a snowman. A built-in LED strip makes the whole sign glow. Stand it on a mantel, a shelf, a sideboard or a table and switch it on.

It runs from a USB port, a phone charger or a power bank. There is an on/off switch on the cable.

### Details

- Free-standing sign: three H letters and three round Os (Santa, elf and snowman)
- Size: approx. 420 mm long x 110 mm high x 40 mm deep
- Lit from the inside by a built-in LED strip
- 5 V USB power. The cable ends in a standard USB-A plug: use a computer USB port, a USB power bank or a phone charger
- On/off switch on the cable
- The cable is fixed to the LED strip inside the sign, so it cannot be unplugged from the sign itself
- The colours come from the plastic itself, with no paint
- 3D printed and assembled by me in Dublin, Ireland
- The phone charger or power bank is not included

### Colours

Shown in the photos: red, green and white H letters with black frames. The Os are Santa, an elf and a snowman. When the light is on, the colours look warmer and brighter than in daylight: the red looks closer to orange and the white looks cream.
