A personalised keyring inspired by Ireland's bilingual road signs. Irish (Gaeilge) on the top line, English below and the distance in km, just like the signs you pass driving through the Irish countryside.

Tell me your destination and distance: a hometown, your partner's name, a wedding venue, an inside joke or even the moon. I design it and print it for you.

### Details

- Size: approx. 75 x 23 mm, plus the key loop
- Bold black frame and raised black lettering on white, in matte PLA plastic
- Metal chain and ring included, ready to clip onto your keys
- Designed and 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland

### Who it is for

- Irish people living abroad who want a piece of home on their keys
- Couples in a long distance relationship (the exact distance between you)
- New car and housewarming gifts
- Stocking fillers, Secret Santa and small souvenirs

### How to personalise

Fill in your details in the order form:

1. Irish name (leave it blank if you do not know it and I will find the traditional Irish version)
2. English place name or your own short text
3. Distance in km

Example: Baile Átha Cliath / DUBLIN / 24

The default colours are white with black text and a black frame, like the real signs. For other colours, send me a message before you order.
