A personalised keyring shaped like an army epaulette, with your rank on the front and your surname in raised white letters along the bottom. It is made for soldiers of the Irish Army, serving or retired. It also suits the family and friends who want to give them something that is not on a shop shelf.

Choose your rank in the order form, tell me your surname and I design and print it for you.

### Details

- Epaulette-shaped keyring with the rank insignia and your surname in raised white lettering
- Grass green body with a dark green frame and white raised details, printed layer by layer in three colours
- Matte PLA plastic
- Silver-coloured metal chain and split ring included
- Designed and 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland
- Size: approx. 65 x 35 mm and 3.7 mm thick

### Ranks available (Irish Army enlisted ranks, OR-1 to OR-9)

- Recruit (no insignia)
- 2 Star Private
- 3 Star Private, Gunner and Trooper
- Corporal
- Sergeant
- Company Quartermaster Sergeant
- Company Sergeant
- Battalion Quartermaster Sergeant
- Battalion Sergeant Major

### How to personalise

1. Choose your rank in the order form.
2. Type your surname into the order form. It prints in capital letters and works best at 10 letters or fewer, like MURPHY.

If your name is longer, message me and I will suggest the best fit before I print.

### Great for

- Promotion and retirement keepsakes
- A gift from the family and friends of a soldier
- Locker keys, kit bags and car keys

### Important

This is an independent, handmade keyring. It is not an official product of the Irish Defence Forces and it is not endorsed by them. It is a keyring, not a uniform item. It is not made to be worn as rank insignia.

Need an Air Corps or Naval Service rank, a different colour or an order for a whole unit? Send me a message before you order.
