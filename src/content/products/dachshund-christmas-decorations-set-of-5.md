A set of five dachshund Christmas tree decorations, all in the same colour. Each one is the long, low outline of a sausage dog in glossy silk plastic, with a white eye, an engraved ear line and, if you want one, a name in raised white letters.

Hang them on the tree, on a wreath or on a garland. You can also tie one to a present. Keep the whole set for yourself or share it out with other dachshund fans.

### Details

- Set of 5 decorations, all the same colour
- Size of each decoration: approx. 75 x 35 mm and 4 mm thick
- Silk PLA plastic for the coloured body, with a white eye, an engraved ear line and an optional name in raised white letters
- Small hanging hole on the back, about 4.5 mm wide. Hooks and ribbon are not included
- Printed in two colours. The colour comes from the plastic itself, with no paint
- 3D printed by me in Dublin, Ireland

### Colours

Choose one of four silk colours in the order form: White, Titanium Steel, Candy Red or Gold. All five decorations in your set come in that colour. Silk plastic has a glossy sheen, so shades can differ a little from your screen to real life.

The eye and the name are always white.

### How to personalise

Type a name into the order form if you want one. The same name is printed on all five decorations. Short names look best, up to about 8 letters. Leave the box empty and you get five plain dachshunds with no name.

### Great for

- Dachshund owners and sausage dog fans
- Christmas trees, wreaths and garlands
- Stocking fillers and Secret Santa gifts

### Care

PLA plastic softens in heat, so keep the decorations away from radiators, open fires, candles and hot bulbs. Wipe them with a damp cloth. The tail is thin, so lift each dog by the body.

Not a toy: keep the set away from young children and pets.
